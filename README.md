## 🎬 How to Open & Watch
You don't need any complex build step or installation:
- **Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/pranav/Desktop/Movie/index.html)** in your file explorer to open it immediately in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- Or run a lightweight local server:
  ```bash
  python -m http.server 3000
  ```
  Then open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

## ✨ Features & Controls

### 1. 📺 YouTube-Style Dedicated Video Watch Stage
- **Grand Centered Theater**: Focused strictly on this video with zero other distractions, recommendations, or comments.
- **Dynamic Ambient Aura Light**: Glowing ambient aura backlight behind the player that breathes smoothly.
  - Choose between 5 customizable moods via the **Aura Mood** button or quick action pill:
    - 🌸 **Sakura Dream** *(Rose & Blush)*
    - 💜 **Lavender Haze** *(Purple & Violet)*
    - 🍑 **Peach Sunset** *(Warm Coral & Amber)*
    - 💖 **Cyber Berry** *(Neon Magenta & Cyan)*
    - 🌌 **Midnight Glow** *(Celestial Aurora)*
    - **Aura Off**
- **Cinema Mode ("Lights Off" • <kbd>C</kbd>)**: Dims the entire page into a 95% dark cinema backdrop with ambient blur so you can focus completely on the movie. Click anywhere or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to exit.
- **Theater Mode (<kbd>T</kbd>)**: Expands the video stage to 100% full-width theater view.
- **Fullscreen Mode (<kbd>F</kbd>)**: Triggers browser full-screen playback.
- **Floating Mini-Player / PiP**: Smoothly minimizes into a floating corner mini-player when you scroll down to read the movie description, with one-tap restore and close buttons.
- **Sleep Timer 🌙**: Set a 15, 30, 45, or 60-minute sleep timer. Screen gently dims when timer expires.
- **Stream Refresh**: Instant reload button if your internet buffers.

### 2. 💖 Aesthetic UI & Interactive Controls
- **Publisher Card**: Features **Pranav ✨** as the official publisher with verified creator badge, subscriber count, and an interactive **Subscribe** button that morphs into *Subscribed 💖* with celebratory particle bursts.
- **Like Button**: Unleashes physics-based floating hearts and sparkles!
- **Share Modal**: 1-click link copy with toast notification + direct share buttons for Instagram, WhatsApp, Pinterest, Telegram, and Twitter/X.
- **Save / Bookmark**: One-tap save to your Jiya TV favorites.
- **Expandable Description**: Clickable timestamps that smoothly scroll to the top of the video stage.

### 3. 📱 Highly Optimized & Responsive for Mobile
- **Responsive Layout**: Designed specifically to look breathtaking on iPhones, Android phones, iPads, tablets, and desktop displays.
- **Touch-Friendly**: 44px+ tap targets, fluid animations, and zero layout shift.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - <kbd>F</kbd>: Fullscreen
  - <kbd>T</kbd>: Theater Mode
  - <kbd>C</kbd>: Cinema Mode (Dim Lights)
  - <kbd>Esc</kbd>: Exit Cinema / Fullscreen / Close Modals
